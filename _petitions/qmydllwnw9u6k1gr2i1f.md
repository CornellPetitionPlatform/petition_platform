Data centers have become a significant threat to our environment, contributing massively to carbon emissions and unsustainable energy consumption. Their demand for electricity often relies on non-renewable resources, which exacerbates climate change and further deteriorates air quality. This increased energy use leads to pollution and destroys natural habitats, contributing to the alarming rate of biodiversity loss.

Additionally, many data centers are located near communities, exposing residents to harmful pollutants and negatively impacting their health and quality of life. The noise, heat, and chemical emissions from these facilities not only poison the air but also contribute to local heatwaves, creating dangerous living conditions for vulnerable populations. It is crucial to prioritize community health and environmental sustainability over unchecked technological expansion.

To protect our planet and our communities, there is an urgent need to ban the construction and operation of new data centers that do not meet strict environmental standards. Embracing greener technologies and promoting energy efficiency is essential for a sustainable future. By taking this stand, we can ensure a healthier planet for generations to come, while also placing people before profit.
