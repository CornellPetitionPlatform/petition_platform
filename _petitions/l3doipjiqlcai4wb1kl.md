The high cost of prescription drugs in the United States has become an alarming issue that affects millions of individuals, forcing many people to skip essential medications. This not only jeopardizes their health but also places a heavy burden on families and the healthcare system as a whole. Everyday citizens are having to choose between paying for life-saving medication and meeting basic living expenses, which is unacceptable in a nation rich in resources.

Forcing individuals to choose between their health and financial stability is not just an economic issue; it directly impacts the quality of life. To address this crisis, Congress must take decisive action by capping drug prices and allowing Medicare to negotiate lower costs for consumers. Implementing these measures would ensure that prescription medications remain affordable and accessible to those who need them most.

By taking these steps, Congress can safeguard the well-being of countless Americans and uphold the principle that healthcare is a right, not a privilege. It is essential to prioritize the health of the nation over corporate profits and ensure that everyone has the opportunity to receive the medications they need without fear of financial ruin.
