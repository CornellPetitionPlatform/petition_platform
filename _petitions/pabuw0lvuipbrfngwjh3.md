The proposed data center in Sahuarita, Arizona poses a significant threat to our local environment and community. Construction and operation of this facility will lead to the disruption of natural habitats and could introduce pollutants that compromise air and groundwater quality. Our community should prioritize sustainable development rather than risking environmental degradation.

 

Moreover, Sahuarita already faces challenges regarding its water resources. The operation of a data center will further strain our already limited water supply, which is essential for both residents and local agriculture. By allowing this project to proceed, we risk jeopardizing our essential water source, impacting the community’s sustainability and quality of life.

 

Protecting Sahuarita's environment and water resources should be our highest priority. The community deserves a future where both nature and sustainable development coexist harmoniously. Stopping the construction of this data center will send a strong message about the importance of prioritizing environmental health and responsible resource management for the benefit of current and future generations.
