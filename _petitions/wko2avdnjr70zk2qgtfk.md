The rise in student anxiety and depression during recent years has reached alarming levels, necessitating immediate action from universities to address this urgent issue. Many students grapple with overwhelming stress, leaving them feeling isolated and unsupported. On-campus mental health counselors are crucial in providing timely assistance, yet many institutions lack the necessary resources to meet the growing demand for these services.

Increased funding for mental health counselors will enable universities to offer more accessible and effective care for their students. This investment not only promotes well-being but also can lead to improved academic performance and retention rates. Faster access to counseling services ensures that students can receive the help they need without prolonged waiting periods, enhancing their overall college experience.

To best support our students, universities should prioritize mental health funding as a critical component of their budgets. By investing in robust mental health services, institutions show a commitment to fostering a safe and healthy learning environment, ultimately equipping students with the tools they need to succeed both academically and personally. Now is the time for universities to make this vital change.
