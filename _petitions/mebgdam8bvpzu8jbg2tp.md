The mental health crisis among university students continues to escalate, with many facing overwhelming challenges and feelings of isolation. Unfortunately, the lack of affordable mental health services on campuses only exacerbates these issues, leaving students without the support they desperately need. This gap in mental health resources can lead to deteriorating academic performance, increased dropout rates, and even tragic outcomes. It is crucial for universities to recognize the urgency of this situation and take action to protect their students' wellbeing.

Expanding free counseling and support programs is an essential step in addressing this pressing concern. Not only would accessible mental health services promote a healthier campus environment, but they would also empower students to seek help without the barrier of cost. By investing in comprehensive mental health resources, universities can foster resilience and academic success among their students, ultimately enhancing the overall educational experience.

Establishing and increasing funding for these programs would demonstrate a commitment to student health and wellbeing. Universities have a responsibility to provide the necessary support systems for their students. By prioritizing mental health services, institutions will not only improve individual lives but also contribute to a more supportive and thriving academic community.
