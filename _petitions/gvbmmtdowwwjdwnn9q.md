Access to affordable healthcare, including both medical and dental services, is a fundamental right that should be available to everyone, regardless of income or socioeconomic status. Many individuals and families are burdened by exorbitant costs, leading to postponing necessary treatments that can worsen health outcomes. Without affordable care, preventable diseases can escalate, causing long-term consequences for both individuals and public health systems.

 

The disparity in healthcare access often disproportionately affects low-income communities, exacerbating existing inequalities. Every person deserves to receive timely care without the fear of incurring insurmountable debt. Affordable healthcare will empower individuals to seek preventive care, ultimately leading to a healthier population and reducing the strain on emergency services.

 

Our society thrives when all members have the opportunity to maintain their health. It is crucial for the government and healthcare institutions to implement policies that prioritize affordability and accessibility in healthcare and dental services. Ensuring that medical and dental care is affordable for all is essential to building a stronger, healthier nation.
