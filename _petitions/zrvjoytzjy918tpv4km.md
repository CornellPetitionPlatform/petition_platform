Data centers are vital to our digital economy, yet many of them rely heavily on fresh water for cooling purposes, often drawing from local sources and putting immense pressure on community water supplies. This unsustainable practice not only threatens the availability of clean water for residents but also contributes to the pollution of local waterways. As climate changes and water scarcity becomes a greater concern, it is imperative that we re-evaluate the operations of data centers.

Communities across the United States deserve a balanced approach to technology that prioritizes environmental responsibility. By reducing the number of data centers that utilize fresh water and promoting innovative cooling technologies that minimize water use, we can protect our valuable water resources. Transitioning to more sustainable practices will not only benefit our environment but also enhance the quality of life for residents who rely on these local water sources.

Taking action now can lead to a healthier future for our communities and ecosystems. Local governments and businesses must collaborate to enforce regulations that limit freshwater usage and encourage the adoption of cleaner, eco-friendly technologies in data centers. Together, we can foster a sustainable technological landscape that does not compromise the health of our communities or the planet.
