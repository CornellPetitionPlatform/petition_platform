The current state of public transportation, particularly bus services, often leaves many individuals frustrated and marginalized. With rising costs making it increasingly difficult to rely on buses as a means of affordable travel, many essential workers and students are put at risk of being unable to access job opportunities, education, and vital services. Enhancing the affordability of bus fare will directly contribute to a more equitable transportation system that allows everyone to thrive.

Reliability is another critical aspect of public transportation that desperately needs attention. Frequent delays and inconsistent schedules deter potential riders and undermine the confidence of current users in the system. Investing in infrastructure and improved scheduling can help restore faith in bus services and encourage an increase in ridership, leading to a more sustainable and efficient transportation network.

Ultimately, making buses more affordable and reliable not only serves to improve the daily lives of countless residents but also supports the overall health and economic vitality of the community. Prioritizing these changes will foster a stronger sense of inclusivity and ensure that everyone has access to convenient and dependable transportation options.
