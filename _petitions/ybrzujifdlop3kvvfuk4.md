Food waste has reached alarming levels in our city, contributing not only to environmental degradation but also to the growing issue of food insecurity among our residents. By establishing an official city division dedicated to minimizing food waste, we can effectively redirect edible surplus from food vendors to those in need. This initiative will not only support vulnerable populations but also foster a culture of sustainability within our community.

 

Many food vendors already produce surplus food that, while still safe and nutritious, often ends up in landfills. Creating a structured program will provide these vendors with a streamlined method to donate excess food. This program would connect them with local shelters, food banks, and community organizations, ensuring that surplus food is utilized meaningfully and efficiently.

 

This initiative is a crucial step in building a fairer, more sustainable city. The establishment of a dedicated division focused on food waste reduction will strengthen the community, enhance food security, and reduce our environmental footprint. It is time to take proactive measures that benefit both our residents and our planet.
