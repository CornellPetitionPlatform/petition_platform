Eviction discrimination remains a pervasive issue affecting countless individuals and families across our community. Those with a prior eviction record often face insurmountable barriers when trying to secure housing, perpetuating a cycle of instability and poverty. This unjust practice disproportionately impacts low-income individuals and marginalized communities, further exacerbating social inequalities.

Legislation is urgently needed to protect renters from unfair discrimination based solely on eviction history. By implementing regulations that limit the use of eviction records in housing applications, we can ensure that everyone has a fair chance at securing safe and stable housing. This change will not only benefit those who have faced eviction but will also contribute to the overall health and resilience of our neighborhoods.

Addressing eviction discrimination is crucial for fostering a more equitable society. Together, we can advocate for change, encouraging lawmakers to recognize the fundamental right to housing for all. Ending this discriminatory practice will help break the cycle of housing instability, allowing individuals and families to rebuild their lives and contribute positively to the community.
