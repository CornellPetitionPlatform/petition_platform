Its unique roasting process also enhances the flavor, providing a nutty and aromatic taste that can appeal to a wide range of palates.

 

Incorporating hojicha into cafeteria beverage options can promote healthier choices among students and staff. Rich in antioxidants, hojicha supports overall wellness and can contribute to a balanced diet. As health-conscious choices become increasingly prioritized, offering such an option can align with nutritional initiatives and foster a culture of wellness within our community.

 

By adding hojicha tea to the menu, cafeterias can provide a delicious and nourishing alternative to sugary drinks and sodas.
