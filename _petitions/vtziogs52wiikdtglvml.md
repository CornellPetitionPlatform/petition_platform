In recent years, our community has witnessed a dramatic increase in rent prices, putting a strain on families and individuals struggling to make ends meet. Many residents are now facing the heartbreaking decision of choosing between paying rent and providing basic necessities. This affordability crisis threatens the fabric of our community, pushing long-term residents out and replacing them with higher-income newcomers, ultimately leading to a loss of cultural diversity and local identity.

Implementing and enforcing rent control is essential to creating a stable housing market that prioritizes residents' needs over profit. By capping rent increases, we can ensure that housing remains accessible for all, allowing families to feel secure in their homes without the constant fear of displacement. Rent control can empower our community and foster a stronger, more connected neighborhood where everyone can thrive.

Taking this vital step will not only alleviate the immediate financial pressure on residents but also promote long-term community stability. A rent control policy can help preserve the character of our local area, keeping it vibrant and inclusive for years to come. It is time to prioritize the well-being of residents and take action against skyrocketing rents that threaten the future of our beloved community.
