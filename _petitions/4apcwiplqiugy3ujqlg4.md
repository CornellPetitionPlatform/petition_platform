The intersection near the middle school poses a significant danger to our children as they travel to and from school. The constant flow of traffic combined with the lack of a designated crossing signal creates a perilous environment for students, who are often seen waiting to cross the street. With many children relying on walking or biking, it is crucial to ensure their safety during peak school hours.

A traffic light at this intersection would provide a clear and safe way for students to cross the road, effectively reducing the risk of accidents. Numerous studies have shown that traffic signals help to manage vehicle flow and improve pedestrian safety, especially in areas frequented by children. Implementing this change would not only protect our kids but also offer peace of mind to parents and guardians concerned about their daily commutes.

Investing in a traffic light at this important intersection represents a necessary step towards safeguarding our community's youth. Enhancing the overall safety of this area will encourage more parents to let their children walk or bike to school, thereby promoting healthier lifestyles and reducing traffic congestion. Let us take action for a safer environment for everyone in our community.
