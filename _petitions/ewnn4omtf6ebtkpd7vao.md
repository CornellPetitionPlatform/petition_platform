The ongoing cost of living increases have become a significant burden for many individuals and families. It is alarming to see that while essential expenses such as housing, food, and healthcare continue to rise, many employers refer to necessary salary adjustments as merit exercises. This misleading terminology undermines the reality that these raises are not merely rewards for exceptional work but essential measures for basic financial stability.

Employers must recognize that employees deserve fair compensation that reflects the economic realities of today. By labeling cost of living adjustments as merit-based increases, it trivializes the need for equitable pay in light of skyrocketing living expenses. Workers are not only striving to keep pace with inflation but also to secure a dignified quality of life without falling into financial distress.

It is crucial to advocate for transparent and fair salary practices that prioritize the well-being of employees over misleading classifications. Employers should commit to providing raises that genuinely reflect the rising cost of living rather than disguising them as discretionary rewards. A fair wage is not just a right but a necessity for fostering a healthier, more productive workforce.
