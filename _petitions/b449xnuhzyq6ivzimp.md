Recent studies have shown that excessive screen time in public schools negatively impacts students' mental health, attention spans, and overall learning experiences. In Kentucky, many classrooms have integrated technology at an alarming rate, with students often spending hours in front of screens rather than engaging in interactive learning methods. This trend raises concerns about the long-term effects on our children’s well-being and education.

 

Reducing screen time in public schools can promote healthier lifestyles and encourage more hands-on learning opportunities. By prioritizing activities such as group projects, physical education, and creative arts, students can develop essential social skills and enhance their cognitive abilities. It’s crucial that educators reassess the reliance on digital tools and find a balanced approach that encourages critical thinking and personal interaction.

 

This petition seeks to advocate for policies that limit screen time and promote alternative teaching methods in Kentucky's public schools. By taking proactive measures, we can create a healthier and more engaging learning environment for our children, fostering their overall development and preserving their love for learning. Let’s invest in a future where education thrives beyond the screen.
