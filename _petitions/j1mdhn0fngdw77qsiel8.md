The mental health of our communities relies heavily on the expertise and dedication of psychiatric nurses. To ensure they are equipped with the most relevant skills and knowledge, it is essential that all psychiatric nurses complete their training in person. In-person training fosters greater engagement and facilitates deeper understanding, enabling nurses to connect with instructors and peers, which ultimately enhances their competency in real-world scenarios.

Additionally, implementing a mandatory number of hours for hands-on practice will significantly improve the quality of care provided to patients. This requirement will ensure that nurses are not only well-versed in theory but also have the practical experience needed to handle complex situations effectively. Increased hours in supervised training will also promote confidence in their abilities, leading to improved patient outcomes and overall satisfaction.

Taking this step is vital for the future of mental health care.
