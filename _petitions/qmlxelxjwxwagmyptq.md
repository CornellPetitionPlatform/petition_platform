The presence of free-roaming cats poses a significant threat to our local bird populations and other wildlife. Cats are natural hunters, and their predatory instincts lead to the deaths of millions of birds each year. In our neighborhood, the delicate balance of the ecosystem is increasingly at risk, and it is vital that we take immediate action to mitigate this impact.

By ensuring all cats are kept indoors, we can protect our local fauna and promote a healthier environment for birds and other wildlife. Indoor cats also benefit from a safer and more enriched living space, reducing risks from traffic, disease, and other dangers. This simple adjustment can strengthen our community's commitment to preserving the beauty and diversity of nature.

Together, we can foster a harmonious relationship between pets and wildlife. Implementing a policy for indoor cats will not only safeguard our cherished bird species but also enhance the overall biodiversity in our neighborhood. Let’s take a stand for our feathered friends and create a safer, more vibrant ecosystem for all.
