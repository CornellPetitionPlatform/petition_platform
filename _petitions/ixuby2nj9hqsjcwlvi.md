Graduate teaching fellows play a crucial role in the academic experience at our institution, yet they often find themselves with little to no say in their class assignments. This situation not only affects the quality of instruction but also undermines the fellows' professional development. Having the opportunity to express preferences based on their academic interests and career goals would lead to a more engaging and effective teaching environment.

Empowering graduate teaching fellows to participate in the class assignment process can result in improved student outcomes. When fellows are able to teach courses aligned with their expertise and passion, they can foster a deeper connection with their students and cultivate a more dynamic classroom atmosphere. Students deserve instructors who are enthusiastic about their subjects, and allowing fellows to choose assignments benefits the entire academic community.

It is essential for our institution to recognize the value of graduate teaching fellows as integral members of the educational framework. Implementing a system that allows these scholars to have input in their class assignments will not only enhance their teaching experience but also contribute positively to the students they serve. This change is necessary for promoting a more equitable and inspiring academic environment for all.
