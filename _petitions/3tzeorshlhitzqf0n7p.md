The proposed data center in our county poses a serious threat to our environment, contributing to increased energy consumption and greenhouse gas emissions. As stewards of our environment, we must prioritize sustainable development that aligns with our values and protects our natural surroundings.

Moreover, contrary to popular belief, data centers do not significantly contribute to job creation in the areas where they are built. Many of the jobs they do provide are low-paying and temporary, failing to deliver the economic boost our community truly needs. Instead, we should focus on attracting industries that offer meaningful, long-term employment opportunities, which will help our residents thrive and contribute positively to our local economy.

By opposing the establishment of this data center, we can advocate for a future that values sustainability, community welfare, and genuine economic growth. It's essential to keep our county a place where residents can enjoy a clean environment and secure jobs. Stand together and demand that our leaders invest in projects that truly benefit our community and the planet.
