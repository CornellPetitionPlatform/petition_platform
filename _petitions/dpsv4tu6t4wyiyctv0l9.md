The mental health crisis among students is a pressing issue that cannot be ignored. Research shows that many young people face significant challenges, including anxiety, depression, and trauma, which can hinder their academic performance and overall well-being. Unfortunately, access to affordable mental health resources in public schools is severely limited, leaving many students without the support they desperately need. It is crucial that local school boards prioritize mental health by funding dedicated full-time counselors in our schools.

Full-time counselors can provide essential services such as individual counseling, crisis intervention, and mental health education. This dedicated support not only helps students navigate their personal challenges but also fosters a healthier school environment. By creating a culture of mental wellness, schools can improve academic performance, reduce absenteeism, and ultimately enhance the overall learning experience. It is imperative to recognize the critical role counselors play in the holistic development of students.

Investing in full-time counselors is an investment in our children's future. Ensuring that every student has access to professional mental health support is essential for their growth and success. By advocating for this funding, we can take a significant step towards ensuring that mental health resources are available and accessible for all students in our community. Together, let’s push for the necessary change that will provide our youth with the support they deserve.
