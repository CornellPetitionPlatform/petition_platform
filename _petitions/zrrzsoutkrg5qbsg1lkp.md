Local parks are cherished spaces for families, children, and pets. However, the increasing number of unleashed dogs poses safety risks and detracts from the enjoyment of these community areas. To address this issue, it is essential that the parks implement clear signage indicating the fines for owners who allow their dogs to roam without a leash. Such measures will not only remind pet owners of their responsibilities but also promote a safer environment for all park visitors.

Having signs prominently displayed throughout our parks will raise awareness about leash laws and the associated fines. By making this information accessible, dog owners will be encouraged to adhere to safety regulations, which will significantly reduce the likelihood of dog-related incidents. Educational signage can also foster a sense of community responsibility, ensuring that all park users feel comfortable and secure while enjoying the outdoors.

Investing in these signs represents a proactive approach to maintaining the harmony of our parks. It signals a commitment to the well-being of both pets and people, ultimately enhancing everyone's experience. Support for this initiative will create safer, more enjoyable spaces for all community members, reinforcing the park's role as a welcoming place for everyone.
