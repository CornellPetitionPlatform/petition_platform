The rampant use of drugs in our streets has reached alarming levels, impacting the safety and wellbeing of our communities. Open drug use not only poses health risks for those directly involved but also creates an unsafe environment for families, children, and pedestrians. It undermines the sense of security that should be a fundamental right for all citizens, eroding trust within neighborhoods and affecting local businesses.

By allowing drug use in public spaces, we enable a cycle of addiction and neglect that compromises the dignity of individuals struggling with substance abuse. Many of these individuals require support and rehabilitation, not exposure to harmful substances in public view. The lack of restrictions and oversight encourages destructive behaviors that deepen the crisis rather than provide pathways for recovery.

;There must be a firm stance against the public use of drugs to reclaim our streets and prioritize health and safety. Establishing stricter regulations will facilitate a community that prioritizes rehabilitation and mental health services. It is essential to foster an environment that nurtures recovery and restores our streets to safe, welcoming places for everyone.
