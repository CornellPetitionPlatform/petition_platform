Making Election Day a federal holiday is essential to ensuring that all citizens can participate in the democratic process without facing barriers. Many individuals work long hours or multiple jobs, making it challenging to find time to cast their vote. By designating Election Day as a holiday, we can promote higher voter turnout and allow everyone, regardless of their work schedule, to have their voices heard in both midterms and general elections.

Other countries have successfully established similar measures, resulting in increased engagement and participation in the electoral process. In the United States, where voter turnout often falls short of the potential, a federal holiday could help create a culture that prioritizes civic duty and responsibility. Making this change would also demonstrate a commitment to democracy and the fundamental right to vote.

It is imperative for lawmakers to take action now and recognize the importance of facilitating participation in our elections. By making Election Day a federal holiday, we will help ensure that every citizen has the opportunity to engage in democracy, thus strengthening the foundation of our nation. This change would be a significant step towards a more inclusive and representative electoral process.
