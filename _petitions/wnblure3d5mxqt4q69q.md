The current tax system burdens individuals and families with various forms of taxation beyond income, placing an unfair strain on hardworking citizens. Taxes on sales, property, and capital gains create a maze of financial obligations that often disproportionately impact lower and middle-income earners. Simplifying the tax structure by focusing solely on income would promote fairness and allow individuals to keep more of their earnings, ultimately fostering economic growth and encouraging spending.

Shifting to an income-only tax model would streamline the tax process, making it more intuitive and less bureaucratic. With a singular focus on income, taxpayers would benefit from reduced compliance costs and greater transparency. This change could also minimize the loopholes and complexities that often lead to tax evasion, ensuring that everyone contributes their fair share based solely on what they earn.

By advocating for this more equitable approach, we can create a tax system that motivates rather than penalizes achievement. A tax structure based on income alone would not only alleviate financial pressure but also encourage a culture of innovation and productivity. It’s time to rethink taxation and embrace a system that truly supports economic empowerment for all.
