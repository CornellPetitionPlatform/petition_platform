Access to clean and well-maintained restroom facilities is essential for all travelers, especially in busy train stations where people often spend long hours waiting for their trains. The lack of accessible and sanitary bathrooms can lead to discomfort, health issues, and an overall negative experience for passengers. By establishing fully monitored and maintained bathrooms within train stations, we can significantly enhance the travel experience for everyone.

  

Implementing this initiative will also promote hygiene and safety, ensuring that travelers have a safe space to use the restroom without worrying about cleanliness or sanitation. Moreover, having staff members or security personnel regularly check and maintain these facilities will help deter vandalism and ensure that the restrooms are welcoming and functional at all times. This commitment to cleanliness reflects a society that values its citizens and their well-being.

  

Investing in adequate restroom facilities speaks to the standard of quality we should uphold in public transportation. It is time for train stations to prioritize this basic necessity, demonstrating that all passengers deserve respect and comfort during their journeys. By advocating for monitored and maintained bathrooms in train stations, we can create a more pleasant and healthy environment for all.
