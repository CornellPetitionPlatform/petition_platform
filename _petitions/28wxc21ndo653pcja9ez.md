Each project results in increased pollution and loss of biodiversity, impacting not only local wildlife but also the well-being of residents who depend on clean air and water. The long-term consequences of this unchecked development could lead to irreversible damage to our environment.

It is crucial to advocate for stricter regulations and sustainable practices within the tech industry. By prioritizing environmentally responsible development, Ohio can foster a balance between technological advancement and ecological preservation. Urgent action is needed to protect our beautiful state from the impending environmental crisis caused by the unchecked proliferation of data centers.
