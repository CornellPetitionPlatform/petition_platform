The safety of our children is paramount, especially when it comes to crossing busy streets near elementary schools. Currently, pedestrian crosswalks located adjacent to these schools lack essential safety measures such as speed bumps and crossing guards. This oversight puts our children at serious risk, as many drivers often exceed speed limits during school drop-off and pick-up times, creating hazardous conditions for young pedestrians.

Implementing speed bumps would effectively slow down traffic in these critical areas, allowing children to cross safely. Additionally, deploying crossing guards during peak hours would provide an extra layer of security, ensuring that our kids can navigate the streets with confidence. As community members, it is vital to advocate for these safety measures to protect our most vulnerable road users.

By prioritizing pedestrian safety near elementary schools, city councils can make a significant impact on the well-being of our children. Taking swift action on this issue not only reflects the community's commitment to safety but also sets a precedent for responsible urban planning. It is imperative that we act now to safeguard our children as they walk to and from school.
