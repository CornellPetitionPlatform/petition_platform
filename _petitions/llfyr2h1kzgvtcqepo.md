The integration of artificial intelligence in educational settings from sixth to twelfth grade poses significant challenges that can hinder genuine learning and personal growth. Students at these critical developmental stages rely on foundational skills, and AI-driven tools can create dependencies that diminish critical thinking and problem-solving abilities. Rather than enhancing academic performance, such tools may actually interfere with the holistic educational experience necessary for the maturation of young minds.

Moreover, the use of AI in classrooms can lead to inconsistencies in assessment and evaluation. Relying on AI-generated answers can result in a lack of accountability and a decline in students' intellectual engagement. As academic integrity becomes challenged, the true potential for growth and understanding diminishes, ultimately impacting score improvements and educational outcomes.

To ensure that students build lasting knowledge and skills, it is essential to prioritize traditional teaching methods that emphasize critical thinking, collaboration, and authentic problem-solving. Banning AI in schools for grades six through twelve will not only foster deeper learning but also prepare students for future challenges in their academic and professional lives. A commitment to genuine educational practices will lead to more meaningful score improvements and a better future for our youth.
