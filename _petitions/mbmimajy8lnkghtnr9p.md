In today's digital age, the pervasive influence of technology and social media has become a double-edged sword, particularly for our youth. While these platforms provide opportunities for connection and learning, they also expose children and teens to cyberbullying and harmful behaviors. Such negative experiences can lead to severe mental health issues, including anxiety and depression, underscoring the urgent need for stronger moderation and protections in the online environment.

It is essential for social media companies to implement stricter policies and monitoring systems to protect young users from bullying and harassment. Enhanced reporting mechanisms, real-time support, and educational resources should be readily available, empowering both parents and children to navigate the online landscape safely. These changes are not just necessary; they are a moral obligation to ensure that technology serves as a force for good, rather than a platform for harm.

By advocating for comprehensive reforms in how technology and social media are moderated, we pave the way for a safer online experience for our youth. Prioritizing mental health and well-being in the digital realm can foster a healthier society, allowing children and teens to thrive without the shadow of bullying. Action is needed now to ensure that future generations can benefit from technology without the threats that currently loom over them.
