This small change can lead to larger impacts on our community’s ecological footprint.

Investing in adequate waste management facilities reflects the city council’s commitment to preserving our parks for future generations. By taking action now, we can enhance our community's quality of life and ensure that our public spaces are clean, safe, and enjoyable for everyone.
