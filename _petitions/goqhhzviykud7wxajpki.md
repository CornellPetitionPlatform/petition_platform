The use of fireworks in New Jersey poses significant risks to public safety and the environment. Every year, countless injuries and fire incidents are reported as a result of improper handling and misuse of fireworks. The loud noises not only cause panic for people but also stress pets and wildlife, harming their well-being and disrupting natural habitats. It is essential to reevaluate the legal use of fireworks to ensure a safer environment for all residents.

Please consider the environmental impact fireworks have, releasing toxic chemicals and contributing to air and noise pollution. During dry periods, they heighten the risk of wildfires, threatening both properties and lives. The aftermath often leaves behind litter and debris that can harm local ecosystems and wildlife. By banning fireworks, New Jersey can lead the way in maintaining cleaner air and safer public spaces.

Ultimately, the celebration and joy associated with fireworks can be achieved through safer, quieter alternatives. Communities can come together with displays that do not compromise safety or well-being. This change would not only protect residents but also ensure that our natural environment is safeguarded for future generations to enjoy. A ban on fireworks in New Jersey is a necessary step toward achieving a safer and more responsible celebration culture.
