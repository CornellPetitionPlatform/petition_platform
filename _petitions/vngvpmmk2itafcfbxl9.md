Access to mental health services is a fundamental right that all university students should enjoy. Unfortunately, many institutions struggle to provide adequate support, leaving students without the necessary resources to cope with the pressures of academic life. By making mental health counseling affordable and accessible, universities can foster an environment where students feel safe to seek help and prioritize their well-being.

Implementing awareness programs is equally essential in promoting mental health support on campuses. These initiatives can educate students about the importance of mental health, reduce stigma, and encourage open discussions. Together, affordable counseling and awareness programs will create a supportive community, empowering students to seek help when needed and enhancing their overall educational experience.

In addition to counseling and awareness programs, universities must invest in hiring more qualified mental health professionals to cater to the growing student population. By prioritizing mental health, universities can not only improve academic performance but also cultivate resilience and personal growth among students, ultimately shaping a healthier, more productive campus culture.
