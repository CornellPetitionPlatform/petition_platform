Adding a dedicated bicycle lane on Main Street is essential for enhancing commuter safety in our community. With increasing numbers of cyclists navigating the busy streets, the current setup poses significant risks to both cyclists and drivers. A designated lane would help reduce accidents and promote a safer environment for all types of commuters.

A bicycle lane would not only protect riders but also encourage more residents to consider cycling as a viable mode of transport. This shift can lead to decreased traffic congestion and lower carbon emissions, contributing to a healthier community and environment. Communities that have implemented similar changes have seen positive outcomes in both safety and ridership levels.

Investing in a bicycle lane on Main Street reflects a commitment to modern urban transportation needs while prioritizing safety for everyone. It’s time to take this step to ensure a safer, more sustainable commuting option for all residents, making our streets accessible and enjoyable for cyclists and drivers alike.
