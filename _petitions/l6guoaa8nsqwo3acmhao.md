The rapid increase in data centers is posing significant threats to our neighborhoods, primarily through heightened pollution and excessive use of local resources. By placing these facilities near residential areas, we risk compromising air quality and increasing noise levels, adversely affecting the health and well-being of our community members. We must prioritize the preservation of our living environment over corporate interests that prioritize profit without consideration of local impacts.

Moreover, the strain on essential resources such as water and electricity cannot be overlooked. Data centers consume vast amounts of energy and water, contributing to shortages that affect our homes, schools, and local businesses. Our neighborhoods deserve to have reliable access to these fundamental resources without the added burden placed by industrial developments designed for profit rather than community benefit.

Local leaders have the power to enact zoning laws and regulations that protect our neighborhoods from the establishment of data centers in close proximity to homes. It is crucial to push for policies that prioritize sustainable and environmentally friendly developments, ensuring that our communities remain healthy and vibrant for all residents. Protecting our neighborhoods is not just about today; it is about securing a livable future for generations to come.
