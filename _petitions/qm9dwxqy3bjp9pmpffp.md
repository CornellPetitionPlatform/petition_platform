Once a video game is purchased, whether from a retail store or an online platform, players should have full ownership of that game. In our increasingly digital world, many games are tied to online accounts, leaving players at the mercy of platform holders who can revoke access or remove games from libraries. This creates an unstable experience and undermines the consumer’s right to fully enjoy their purchased products.

 

Owning a game should mean that players have the freedom to play it at any time, regardless of shifting corporate policies or server availability. Just like physical copies, digital ownership should not be contingent upon the whims of companies. This lack of true ownership diminishes the value of the game and erodes the trust between gamers and publishers.

 

Establishing clear guidelines that recognize consumers' rights to ownership in the digital space can foster a healthier gaming ecosystem. This change will not only benefit players, but it will also promote fairness and accountability among game developers and retailers. Let’s advocate for a more equitable gaming environment where purchased games truly belong to the players.
