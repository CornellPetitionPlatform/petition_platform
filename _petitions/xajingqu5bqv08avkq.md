Survivors of sexual assault deserve comprehensive support and a safe environment at Cornell University, yet many feel left behind in the current system. It is crucial for the university to enhance its resources, including counseling services, support groups, and educational programs focused on prevention and awareness. By creating a robust support network, Cornell can help survivors heal and empower them to share their experiences without fear of stigma.

Accountability is another essential aspect that needs urgent attention. The procedures for reporting and addressing sexual assault cases must be transparent, fair, and efficient. Implementing stricter consequences for perpetrators while ensuring that survivors receive the protection and respect they deserve will send a clear message that Cornell University prioritizes safety and justice on campus.

Enhancing support for survivors and holding individuals accountable will foster a safer and more inclusive environment for all students. By taking meaningful action, Cornell can establish itself as a leader in addressing sexual assault and supporting those affected, ultimately creating a campus culture that prioritizes respect, dignity, and safety.
