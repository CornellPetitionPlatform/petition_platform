Access to nutritious meals is essential for the health and academic success of children. Many families struggle to provide adequate meals, especially those facing financial hardships. By ensuring that all children enrolled in public schools receive free breakfast and lunch, we can combat hunger and promote equal opportunities for learning, allowing every child to thrive regardless of their economic background.

The current system, which often requires parents to pay for school meals, places an unfair burden on low-income families and can lead to stigma for those who cannot afford to pay. Providing universal access to free meals not only alleviates this financial pressure but also creates a supportive environment in which every student feels valued and included. Healthy, well-nourished students are more likely to participate actively in their education and achieve higher academic performance.

Investing in free breakfast and lunch programs is a proactive step toward building a healthier, more equitable future for our children. Policymakers must prioritize this initiative to ensure that every child has access to the nutrition they need to succeed. By supporting free meals for public school students, we can foster a more inclusive educational system and contribute to the well-being of our communities.
