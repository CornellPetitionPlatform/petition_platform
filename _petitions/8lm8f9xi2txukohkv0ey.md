The safety of students and staff should be the top priority in any educational institution. Unfortunately, the presence of unauthorized individuals, such as homeless people, on our campus has raised serious concerns among students and parents alike. These unwelcomed visitors can create an uncomfortable environment and pose potential safety risks. Implementing better security measures can help ensure a safe learning atmosphere for all students.

Enhanced security, including the presence of school resource officers, surveillance cameras, and controlled access points, would not only deter unwanted visitors but also provide a quicker response to any incidents that may occur. Such measures have proven effective in other schools, significantly reducing the number of unauthorized individuals wandering the premises. A secure environment allows students to focus on their education without fear or distraction.

By prioritizing security improvement, the school can demonstrate its commitment to protecting its students and staff. The community deserves a safe and welcoming educational environment. Let’s take action now to implement these important changes and promote a school atmosphere that fosters both learning and safety.
