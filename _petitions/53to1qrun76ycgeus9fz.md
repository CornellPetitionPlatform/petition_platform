In particular, the lack of affordable tutoring services in mathematics can severely impact a child's ability to grasp essential concepts and keep pace with their peers. This gap in support can result in long-term disadvantages, affecting their self-esteem, confidence, and future educational opportunities.

 

By offering free maths tutoring in schools, educators can provide valuable assistance to those who need it most. These programs would not only help students improve their skills but also promote a more inclusive learning environment. Every child, regardless of their financial background, should have the opportunity to achieve their full potential and thrive academically.

 

Implementing such initiatives would demonstrate a commitment to equity in education, showcasing that all students truly deserve an equal chance to succeed. It is essential for schools to recognize the importance of equal access to resources and support, ensuring that every student can excel in mathematics and beyond.
